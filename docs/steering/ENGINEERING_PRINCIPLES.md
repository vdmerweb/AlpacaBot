# Engineering Principles

## Purpose

Describe how engineers should think when designing and building AlpacaBot.

## Principles

- Keep modules small, cohesive, and easy to reason about.
- Prefer explicit interfaces over implicit dependencies.
- Optimize for readability and maintainability over cleverness.
- Minimize dependencies and avoid unnecessary abstractions.
- Design for extension, not modification.
- Fail fast with meaningful errors.
- Avoid premature optimization.
- Automate repetitive tasks where it reduces risk.
- Document the rationale behind important decisions.
- Preserve architectural boundaries as the system evolves.
