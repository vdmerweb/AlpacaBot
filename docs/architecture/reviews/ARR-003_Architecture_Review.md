# ARR-003 – Architecture Review

## Final Observation

One aspect of this project has stood out consistently: the quality of collaboration.

The Product Owner has maintained a clear long-term vision and resisted the temptation to rush into coding.

The Implementation Engineer has shown an increasing ability to implement within architectural boundaries rather than independently defining them.

Our review process has created a constructive feedback loop in which architecture informs implementation, implementation validates architecture, and both feed improvements back into the steering library.

This is the hallmark of a disciplined engineering process.

I am confident that if we continue working in this manner, AlpacaBot will not simply become an application that connects to Alpaca. It will become a well-architected, extensible trading platform capable of supporting additional brokers, trading strategies, automation, and future AI-assisted development without requiring fundamental architectural redesign.
